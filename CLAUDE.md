# goa-console — DIY 24+ channel line-level mixing console

Owner: Ruslan. Answer in English. Expert electronics hobbyist (KiCad, analog audio) — be technical, no hand-holding.

## PCB scope
Component placement only. Ruslan does all copper routing in KiCad. Deliver no tracks,
vias or copper zones; do not autoroute, pour grounds or add stitching/escape vias.
Keep footprint pads and the net-tie's internal copper. See `AGENTS.md`.

## What this is
A Soundcraft-Delta-style console for mixing hardware synths (goa trance).
Line level only. One identical main PCB per channel (plus a small jack board), joined by a 34-way IDC
ribbon "motherloom".
Channel = a Soundcraft Delta Theatre input without the mic stage (decided 2026-10-22): line gain,
Ø, HPF, 4-band EQ, post-EQ insert, ON, PFL, aux 1-6 with PRE switches, pan + PAN switch, routing,
direct out. Strip spec v4.0, schematics and placement (main board + jack board) done.

## Files
- `channel_strip.kicad_sch` / `.kicad_pro` — channel strip schematic v4.0 (KiCad 7 file format, opens in KiCad 10; User-size sheet, label-connected).
  GENERATED by `tools/gen.py` — edit the generator (netlist is in the `P(...)` calls), then regenerate.
- `master.kicad_sch` / `.kicad_pro` — master section (one User-size sheet, ~1.0 x 2.3 m, 1059 parts).
  GENERATED by `tools/gen_master.py` from master_section_spec.md (helper functions per circuit;
  refs auto-assigned, dual op-amps/comparators allocated unit by unit with their decoupling).
  Both generators use the shared builder `tools/schgen.py`. Same loop with `master` in place of
  `channel_strip` (`tools/gen_master.py master.kicad_sch`). Exports: master.net/.pdf/_BOM.csv.
  The master's parts, reference allocation and circuit functions live in `tools/master_lib.py`
  (shared with gen_jig.py); gen_master.py is just the master's netlist.
- `jig_master_stub.kicad_sch` / `jig_load_board.kicad_sch` (+ .kicad_pro) — test-jig boards (test_jig_spec.md
  v0.2), GENERATED by `.venv/bin/python tools/gen_jig.py` (writes both, plus `jig_*_parts.json`: blocks and
  decoupling pairs for placement). Same verify/check_fp/ERC loop. PCBs (placement only):
  `$KPY tools/place_jig.py` → `jig_master_stub.kicad_pcb`, `jig_load_board.kicad_pcb`; parity DRC in place.
- Placement engine `tools/placer.py` (`place(net, out, layout)`: fixed parts, zone packer, near-placement,
  silk labels and references); `tools/place_strip.py` and `tools/place_jig.py` are just layouts.
- `goa-console.kicad_sym` + `sym-lib-table` — project symbols (DRV134).
- `jack_board.kicad_sch` / `.kicad_pro` — strip jack board (J1, J2, J5 NJ6FD-V, C8/C9, CN5 2x5 link),
  GENERATED by `tools/gen.py` together with channel_strip.kicad_sch (link pinout = `LINK` in gen.py, spec 16.4).
  Exports: jack_board.net/.pdf/_BOM.csv. Same verify/check_fp/ERC loop with `jack_board` in place of `channel_strip`.
- `channel_strip_v4.kicad_pcb` — strip v4.0 main board, 38 × 400 mm (y 44–444) at 11.5 mm under an 8 HP × 12U
  panel (two pot columns), 176 parts + 6 M3 holes; `jack_board.kicad_pcb` — 38 × 43 mm (y 0–43) at ~24 mm
  under the panel. Same y frame, so they overlay. Component placement only: no tracks, vias or copper zones.
  Rebuild both with `tools/build_strip_pcb.sh [main.kicad_pcb [jack.kicad_pcb]]` or, per board,
  `/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/3.9/bin/python3 tools/place_strip.py channel_strip.net channel_strip_v4.kicad_pcb`

## Decisions made (do not silently change)
- Strip panel (2026-10-22): 8 HP × 12U Eurorack-style, two pot columns, jacks on the panel (Neutrik
  NJ6FD-V); custom frame with rails only at the panel ends.
- PSU (2026-10-22): option B, two isolated LRS-150-24 at 21.6 V → 10 µH / 2200 µF → LM1084-ADJ (17.0 V,
  47 µF ADJ soft start) as identical floating rails; TL431 + PNP + 2N6505G SCR crowbar at 19.1 V per rail.
- Power (2026-10-22): strip v4.0 draws ~85 / 72 mA, console ~2.65 / 2.23 A typical (≤ 3.8 A on +V).
  PSU 4 A per rail; inlet 7-pin XLR NC7MD-LX (panel, two 5 A contacts per rail, wired to a 7-way terminal);
  wing PTCs Bourns MF-RX160/72 (72 V: a pin 31/32 short puts 34 V across one PTC). Master spec 24–27.
- Mid-band tuning (2026-10-22): the Delta's Wien band-pass with a 100K C-law dual (not stocked) is
  replaced by a KHN state-variable band-pass with the same response (peak 0.407, Q 0.275), tuned by a
  stocked Alpha A10K dual as dividers (430R / 13K wiper-to-top / 4K7) into the two integrators.
  CW = higher frequency, within 0.28 oct of a log sweep. U12–U14 OPA1612. A10K, not A100K: 100K was
  5.7–8.6 dB noisier. Spec 6.2.
- Routing (2026-10-22): the PVA2 routing switches carry logic only. Pole A drives a DG413 (U9–U11, at the
  bus header) that moves each 4K7's bus end between the bus (D side) and AGND; pole B drives a yellow
  routing LED. VL = +5VL from +VA via 4K7 + BZX84C5V1. Replaces the 4PDT idea (no vertical part exists).
- Jack board (2026-10-22): a 1/4" plug goes ~30 mm deep, so J1/J2/J5 sit on a separate 38 × 43 mm board
  ~24 mm under the panel, linked to the main board by a 2x5 IDC ribbon (CN5 ↔ CN3, both bottom side).
- Delta clone without mic stage (2026-10-22), replacing "no EQ, no trim". Input = our receiver +
  separate inverting gain stage (Delta line topology rejected, 4.7 dB noisier); HPF unity-gain
  Butterworth 87 Hz (Delta's has +3 dB); EQ with Delta values. Then: line gain −15 to +15 dB
  (centre detent 0 dB), Ø switch, switched HPF and 4-band EQ as the Delta (OPA1612), EQ IN, insert
  post-EQ (jumper for pre-EQ), ON switch with green LED (replaces MUTE), PAN switch, routing LEDs,
  PRE switch per aux pair (jumper: pre-fader / pre-EQ), direct out (aux 6 doubles as its level via
  DIR). Internal level stays 0 dBu; 4K7 summing stays.
- One 34-pin header per strip on a daisy-chained ribbon with crimped sockets (decided 2026-10-19;
  CN2 dropped).
- Bus = real Delta motherloom pinout (read from Delta ED3344C): GRP1-4 = SUB1-4 on pins 15/13/11/9,
  MIX R 17, MIX L 18, AUX6..1 on 20-25 (AUX1=24, AUX2=25), GND CANCEL 26, PFL 27, PFL DET 29,
  -V 30-31, +V 32-33, +48 V pin 34 not connected. Spares: 1,3,5,7,19. Even pins 2-16 = GND.
- Ground cancel: 10 Ω from strip AGND to pin 26; master summing amps reference that line.
  Each master summing amp is followed by a unity differential re-inverter (sum − GCANCEL).
  No tie from GCANCEL to master ground at the master end (2026-10-19; sim: a 22 Ω tie costs ~45 dB).
  Replaces SSL-style impedance-balanced bus (dropped unless the test jig says otherwise).
- Power on the ribbon (2 conductors per rail); heavy ground via a 6.3 mm spade per strip.
- MIX/SUB summing resistors 4K7 (master feedback must also be 4K7). AUX/PFL 22K.
  (10K would keep real Delta modules compatible; 4K7 is ~3 dB quieter.)
- Fader post-amp +10 dB (R38 2K2; omit for unity). Hot synths >~+12 dBu clip with fader at top.
- Channel path is non-inverting (tip = hot). Master summing inverts → master must re-invert once.
- Routing switch off = resistor's bus end to clean ground (Delta practice).
- AC coupling 220 µF NP + 47K on every node feeding the bus; JFET (J112) soft mute;
  separate logic rails +VL/−VL and logic ground GNDD joined to GNDA at one point (NT1).
- OPA1612 in noise-critical stages. NE5534 good for sub/aux summing (low current noise).
- 4 subs (with inserts), 6 aux (AFL on masters), 4 stereo returns, PFL only (no SIP), mute relay on mains.
- SSL bus compressor (Gyraf clone) as an external unit on the master insert.
- All balanced outputs use TI DRV134 (2026-10-19), Sense pins after a 10 µF NP per leg, mute
  relay after that. Replaced THAT1646, which went EOL (THAT memo 2026-09-01). The same memo
  kills THAT 2180/2181 (bus-compressor VCA, strip VCA option) and THAT 124x.
- VCA (decided 2026-10-19): Sound Semiconductor SSI2162 for the bus compressor (sidechain
  rescaled 6.1 → 33 mV/dB; THAT2181 backorder route rejected) and as a plug-in module for the
  strip VCA option.

## Verified so far
- Strip v4.0 with jack board, DG413 routing and KHN mid bands (2026-10-22): netlists main 697 / jack 30
  pin entries, 0 mismatches; check_fp 227 + 6 parts, 0 problems; ERC 0 on both; tools/sim_netlist.py 30/30
  through the ribbon (levels, gain, Ø, HPF, every EQ band incl. mid-rotation, bypass, mute, direct out at J5,
  aux pre/post, PAN, PFL, routing via the DG413s incl. off = silent);
  with ribbon conductor 6 removed, 23 checks fail, so the link is really exercised.
- Mid tuning sim (`sim/mid_tuning.py`): KHN + A10K vs Delta Wien + C100K, both in the U6A stage: same range
  (684 Hz–10.47 kHz / 148 Hz–2.29 kHz), ±14.6 dB, Q 1.42–1.46 (Delta 1.40–1.45); noise 1.3 / 0.6 dB higher.

  +20 dBu (−67 dB if the switch were at the source end), no summing-amp peaking up to 2.2 nF of bus
  capacitance, switching click −55 dBu peak in the 20 kHz band, crosstalk from an unassigned strip −111 dB at 20 kHz.
- Strip v4.0 placement (KiCad 10.0.7, 2026-10-22): main 227 footprints + 6 holes, jack board 6 + 2 holes;
  0 tracks/vias/zones, 0 schematic-parity issues. Main board: 0 violations, including silkscreen (refs

  holes, 0 tracks/vias/zones, 0 schematic-parity issues; no clearance, courtyard, hole or
