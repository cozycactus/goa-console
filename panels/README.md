# Front panels: fabrication drawings

Generated by `.venv/bin/python tools/panel_fab.py` (needs `fonttools` in the venv and `rsvg-convert`) from
`tools/panel_layout.py`. The 3D model (`tools/console_3d.py`) builds its panels from the same layout, so the
drawings, the model and the placed boards can't disagree. Re-run after any placement change, once `render/*.json`
has been re-exported.

## Files

- **`.dxf`** (R12, mm): origin at the bottom-left corner, seen from the front. Layers:
  - `OUTLINE`: the plate;
  - `CUT`: every hole and the fader slot;
  - `LEGEND`: the print as closed outlines, text included. Fill them; no font is needed.
- **`.svg` / `.pdf`:** 1:1 artwork, 1 unit = 1 mm. Plate and cut-outs are red hairlines; the print is black fill, as a
  single ink layer. Send the PDF for UV or screen printing; the strip and group PDFs carry no number.
- **`_numbers.pdf`:** the same artwork with the strip number (1–24) or "GROUP n" at the bottom, one page per panel.
- **`.png`:** a preview, cream ink on black. The finish and ink colour are placeholders.
- **`_holes.csv`:** every hole: x and y from the bottom-left corner, diameter or slot size, and the part it serves.
  It also lists the board standoffs' positions as "M3 blind stud, back side, not through" (DXF layer STUDS: a circle
  and cross each).
  - **When they apply:** only if the maker offers welded M3 studs on the back of 1.5 mm anodised aluminium
    (`met1_ret1_review.md` P4).
  - **Pressed-in studs won't do:** PEM's concealed-head clinch studs need a 1.60 mm sheet.
  - **The jack-board studs:** they also bond CHASSIS to the panel, so they need bare metal contact.
  - **Checked:** every stud's 6 mm area stays 1 mm or more from every front cut-out.
- **Without welded studs:** the jack-board standoffs get countersunk M3 screws instead: Ø3.4 through, 90° to Ø6.5
  (DXF layer CSK_NO_STUDS; hole-table rows "ONLY IF NO WELDED STUDS"). Every countersink clears the cut-outs and the
  print, the tightest being group H1, its head 0.78 mm from the RET 1 title.
  - **Ordering:** a maker who can't weld studs drills these; one who can ignores them.
  Use it to check a maker's quote or a first article.

**Checks** (`panel_fab.py` runs them every time; all three panels pass):
- at least 1.0 mm of web between cut-outs;
- at least 1.0 mm from any cut-out to the edge;
- no cut-out over a frame rail;
- the print at least 0.2 mm clear of every cut-out and 0.3 mm inside the plate. The section rules run full width,
  ending 0.41 mm from each edge, as the Delta's do.

## Material and print

- **Material:** 1.5 mm aluminium, anodised. Black is the placeholder; the 3D model uses it.
- **Print:** one ink, the Delta's cream (`panel_print.md`).
- **Fine features** (check the printer can hold them; makers compared in `panel_makers.md`):
  - smallest text 1.5 mm em, about 1.1 mm caps;
  - dots 0.54 mm square;
  - fader ticks 0.36 mm, rules 0.28 mm.

## Open before ordering

- **Fader scale.** The dB marks follow an assumed two-slope A law (`panel_layout.fader_theta`). Measure a real
  PTB0143 (a few points of resistance against travel) and update the law before printing. The bottom marks crowd
  under this law: where they come closer than 1.2 mm, ticks are dropped, but ∞ always prints.
- **Sample checks** (`panel_parts_drawings.md`):
  - PV cap clearance in the Ø8.0 hole;
  - PTB pin side and slot fixings on a 1:1 print;
  - M9 nut thickness on the RK09L;
  - the Alpha bushing's thread (no spare thread with the 1.5 mm spacer).
- **Knobs:** Elma Ø14.5 collet knobs (`knob_proposal.md`). The dot rings and numerals are drawn for that diameter. A
  numeral that would come within 0.5 mm of the plate edge, or within 1.2 mm of another knob's numeral, is left out.
