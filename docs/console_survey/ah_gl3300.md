## 2. Published specs
None in this document. The only levels are annotations on the schematics:
- master insert send, monitor feed and C3101 meter feed: **−2 dBu** (p.16, p.19);
- PFL/AFL sums: **−8 dBu** (p.19);
- group / aux master post-amps: "+10dBu" (p.13).

This suggests an internal level of about −2 dBu, like the Delta's. Noise, CMRR, crosstalk and headroom are not stated.

## 3. Per topic

**T1 Line input.** **Read** (p.2, p.4):
- There is no separate line receiver. The TRS line jack (ferrite beads FB19/20) feeds R10/R11 10K 1 % per leg into the mic preamp's input. S2 selects mic or line.
- The preamp is a discrete 2N4403 pair with TL072 IC2A, after 100 µF/25 couplers C9/C10, with R97 2K2 across the legs and R29/R30 8K2 from each leg to ground.
- **Inferred:** the line pad is about −21 dB differential but only −7 dB common-mode. The 1 % pad therefore costs CMRR and adds the pad's noise ahead of the gain stage.
- The stereo channel uses the same scheme with 8K2 pads (p.6).
- vs ours: different, and ours is better for line sources (buffered receiver, 54 dB against a 1K source). Nothing to take.

**T2 Gain.** **Read:** the mic-pre gain is a 10KC dual, VR7, in the emitter and op-amp legs. The gain range is not stated.
- vs ours: different. Theirs is a mic-pre range; ours is a separate ±15 dB line stage.

**T3 Polarity.** **Read:** S3 swaps the legs after S2, ahead of the couplers and the preamp.
- vs ours: the same idea (legs swapped ahead of the diff amp).

**T4 HPF.** **Read:** "LO-CUT" S4.
- **In:** IC2A → C14 330 nF → node shunted by R19 4K7 → C15 47 nF / C12 47 nF Sallen-Key on IC2B (TL072), with R21 15K feedback and R20 100K to ground.
- **Out:** C14 → IC2B, used as a follower (4.8 Hz with R20).
- **Inferred:** the Sallen-Key alone is 87 Hz with Q ≈ 1.3, plus a first-order 103 Hz pole (330 n / 4K7). Overall it is a fixed, roughly 3rd-order cut near 100 Hz.
- vs ours: different. Ours is a sweepable 12 dB/oct, Q 0.707.

**T5 EQ.** **Read:**
- Four bands on one TL072 (IC1): HF shelf (VR1 20KB, C3 1n), MF1 500 Hz-15 kHz and MF2 35 Hz-1 kHz (200KC duals), LF shelf (VR4 20KB, C8 0.33 µF).
- Stability caps: 22 pF / 22 nF.
- EQ IN switch S6 chooses between the EQ output (C16 47 µF) and the pre-EQ signal (C34 47 µF).
- vs ours: a similar 4-band layout. Ours uses OPA1612 and narrower mid ranges. Mid tuning is skipped as instructed.

**T6 Insert.** **Read:**
- Position: post lo-cut, **pre-EQ**.
- Send: IC2B → C27 100 µF → R61 22R, with R63 100K bleed.
- Return: C18 47 µF → R31 22K → straight into the EQ (R3 47K). There is no return buffer.
- Normalling: the return is normalled to the send on the TRS jack contact (p.4). Unbalanced.
- vs ours: different position (ours is post-EQ, with a pre-EQ jumper). Ours buffers and filters the return; theirs does not.

**T7 Mute.** **Read:** J111 JFET shunts (TR5, TR6), each after a series resistor.
- **TR6** sits on the fader wiper, after R51 5K6.
- **TR5** sits in a separate pre-fade buffer, IC4B 5532: R50 10K in, R49 10K to ground, gain about ×2.
- Gate drive: TR7 2N4403 swings MUTE between −VA (off) and ~0 V (on) through R92 100K / C35 100 nF. That is a 10 ms ramp.
- Drive sources: the panel key S7 (R96 470K to −VA) or **4 mute-group buses** on the ribbon (pins 31-34) through 1N4148 diodes D4-D7 and keys S15-S18. TR2 BC549 lights two red LEDs.
- **Read:** both mute stages also feed a scaled copy of their own input into a 150R in the op-amp's ground leg:
  - fader stage: C37 + R28 22K + R24 1K8 from the wiper into R35 150R;
  - pre-fade stage: R98 47K into R56 150R.
- **Inferred:** this is a null for the JFET's on-resistance leak. The fader stage cancels at Ron ≈ 26 Ω, the pre-fade stage at ≈ 16 Ω.
- vs ours: the same JFET-shunt principle. Ours uses two J112s in cascade (≈ 86 dB) rather than one shunt plus a JFET-dependent null. Ours also mutes the pre sends and the direct out; theirs mutes the pre sends by default (J28 "POST-MUTE" fitted), and the direct out only when it is taken post-fade.

**T8 Fader.** **Read:**
- **Channel:** 10KD fader → R51 5K6 → C25 47 µF → IC4A 5532 +, with R37 47K bias, R58 5K6 feedback / 22 pF, and R44 2K2 + R35 150R to ground. That is about +10.6 dB.
- **Inferred:** the 5K6 + 47K load costs the wiper about 1 dB, so the net gain is about +9.6 dB.
- **Master** (p.16): RV1 10KD → R31 47R → C9 → IC3 +, with R35 10K / R33 4K7 + R34 10R. That is +9.9 dB ("+10dB").
- **Master, "FADER SHUTOFF COMPENSATION":** R30 100K from the fader's top into R34 10R in the post-amp's ground leg (J9 disables it).
- **Inferred:** it injects −2×10⁻⁴ (−74 dB) of the top signal to cancel what leaks through the fader's ground-end resistance.
- Group / aux masters: 10KA / 20KK pot into an inverting 5532 stage, 12K in / 39K feedback, +10.2 dB.
- vs ours: the same "fader then +10 dB amp" scheme. Ours loads the wiper with 100K. We have no shut-off compensation.

**T9 Pan.** **Read:**
- VR17 10KAC dual, passive. It is fed from IC4A through C28 100 µF and drives the routing resistors directly, with no buffers.
- The L wiper feeds L mix (R73 10K) and odd groups (22K); the R wiper feeds R mix and even groups.
- The pan is always in circuit; there is no PAN switch.
- vs ours: different. Ours buffers both wipers and has the PAN switch.

**T10 Aux.** **Read:**
- Eight aux sends, 20KK pots, R36 etc. 12K into the A1-A8 buses.
- Per-aux 3-way links P / S / A:
  - P: pre-fade;
  - S: switched by the PRE/POST key, S8 for aux 1-4, S9 for aux 5-8;
  - A: post-fade.
- Pre-fade source:
  - J28 post-mute (fitted) or J29 pre-mute;
  - J30 pre-EQ / J31 post-EQ (post-EQ fitted).
- Aux master: virtual earth with 18K // 22 pF (12K in → +3.5 dB). It is DC-coupled at the sum (FB1 ferrite), with 47 µF after it.
- vs ours: equivalent. They have links per aux; we have a key per pair plus a jumper.

**T11 Routing.** **Read:** S10 L-R and S11-S14 (1-2 … 7-8) switch each summing resistor's bus end between the bus and a common line tied to channel 0V.
- vs ours: the same principle (Delta practice, constant source load). Theirs carries audio through the key; ours does it with a DG413.

**T12 Mix summing.** **Read:**
- **L/R** (p.16): L_MIX → FB1 → **C15 220 µF/10 in series with the virtual earth** → IC1 5532 with R26 8K2 // 100 pF, R25 100K bus bleed.
- Gains: channels 10K → −1.7 dB; group returns 8K2 → unity.
- The sum is followed by a TL072 unity re-inverter (10K / 10K // 100 pF), then the insert send (R36 22R, −2 dBu).
- **Groups** (p.13): 22K into 18K // 22 pF, again with a 220 µF/10 V cap in series with the summing node and 100K bleed.
- vs ours: the same virtual-earth summers. Our 4K7 / 4K7 is quieter.
  - **Inferred:** their noise gain is about 35 at 32 channels + 8 groups.
  - Theirs blocks DC at the summing node, which makes the summer's DC gain 1. Ours AC-couples every source.

**T13 Bus ground.** **Read:**
- The summer + inputs go to local 0V. There is **no ground-cancel line**.
- Each channel's 0V reaches the ribbon on **one** conductor (pin 30, via link J34 "FIT"). Each module also has a tag P1 / P17 "TC EARTH BUS" (tinned-copper earth bus).
- Rails: 10R + 47 µF per rail per module.
- Master connector board revision 2 is titled "EARTHING SPLIT".
- vs ours: ours is stronger. We use ground cancel + re-inverter, several ribbon grounds and a 14 AWG harness. Their TC earth bus does the same job as our G1 harness.

**T14 AC coupling.** **Read:** 47-100 µF electrolytics everywhere with 100K bleeds, and 220 µF/10 V at each summing node. **Inferred:** polarised parts, no film.
- vs ours: we use NP MUSE ES and film. Nothing to take.

**T15 PFL / AFL.** **Read:**
- **Channel:**
  - S5 pole A switches R26 22K's bus end between 0V and PFL_MIX (post-EQ, pre-mute, pre-fader).
  - Pole B pulls PFL_DC to 0V through R27 15K and lets LD6 light from a local R33 8K2.
- **Master** (p.19):
  - PFL_DC is pulled up by R66 4K7 through R67 560R / C33 1 µF.
  - Q9 2N4403 detects the line, and Q8 does the same for AFL_DC.
  - D13/D14 and Q10 drive the "PAFL ACTIVE" LED.
  - D16 drives a CD4053 (IC8) that switches monitors, phones and meters.
- PFL / AFL sums: virtual earth through 220 µF, PFL 8K2 (−8.6 dB → "−8dBu"), AFL 10K.
- **Inferred:** the 4053 runs on about ±7.7 V, divided from ±VA by 4K7 / 10K / 4K7 with 47 µF. That is why PFL is carried at −8 dBu.
- vs ours: the same architecture: a DC detect line plus an electronic switcher. Ours uses a DG413 on full rails, so PFL keeps full headroom. We also have a 100K-per-strip detect line, a clamp and a flasher.

**T16 Direct out.** **Read:**
- Source: J33 post-fade (IC4A output, muted) or J32 pre-fade (post-EQ, pre-mute).
- Then R57 47R → TRS tip through 0R, ring through 47R to 0V (p.4). This is impedance-balanced.
- vs ours: the same scheme (75R / 75R). Theirs has no level control.

**T17 Master outputs.** **Read:**
- L/R post-amp → C14 47 µF → **cross-coupled balanced driver**: a 5532 pair, R70/R81 4K7 and R71/R80 10K 1 %, 22 pF, 47R build-outs. The cross-feed resistors take their feedback after the build-outs.
- **Inferred:** about +6.6 dB differential, so −2 dBu becomes about +4 dBu, and the output is floating.
- XLR board: 10R series and 47 µF per leg, 2K2 1 % per leg to 0V on the driver side, 100K bleed.
- Mono: a 4K7 summer from post-fade L / R (links J5-J8). S6 selects "FOH mode" (L+R sum) or "monitor mode" (AFL/PFL).
- No mute relays and no power-on mute circuit are shown.
- vs ours: different. The DRV135 is the integrated form of the same cross-coupled topology. Our relay mute with a power-on delay is something theirs lacks.

**T18 Meters.** **Read:**
- **Channel:** LM339 with three LEDs from one tap (post-EQ, pre-fader).
  - Detector: R66 22K + 1N4148 into C29 1n5 to −VA, R70 2M2.
  - Ladder from +VA: R65 10K, R77 5K6, R78 680R, R79 180R.
  - PEAK and "0dB" compare the detector against the ladder; SIGNAL compares the raw signal through R67 1M. C38 1 µF on the SIGNAL comparator output stretches the green LED.
  - **Inferred:** thresholds are 0.40 / 0.064 / 0.011 × VA. At an assumed ±15 V the PEAK LED lights at about +13.6 dBu (≈ 6 dB below clip), with no hold (2M2 × 1n5 ≈ 3 ms), and SIGNAL at about −17 dBu.
- **Groups:** four LEDs (PEAK / +6 / 0 / SIGNAL), with the same scheme.
- **Master** (p.17, p.20):
  - Precision half-wave rectifier on a TL072: 47K in, 330K feedback, ×7.
  - R93 1K8 into C6 1 µF. **Inferred:** 1.8 ms attack, ≈ 330 ms decay through the 330K.
  - LM339 ladder (150R … 2K2) from +VA through 3K7.
  - 12-LED series string (−26 to +12) fed by a 2N4403 current source (R95/R96 1K2), each comparator shorting its LED.
- vs ours: master: the same LED-string / current-source architecture as ours. Strip: ours is better (five taps, both polarities, 150 ms hold).

**T19 Power.** **Read:**
- Module rails: R80 / R81 10R "PF" + 47 µF/25 per rail on the channel. **Inferred:** "PF" probably means a fusible resistor.
- Ribbon: two pins per rail (5/6 −V, 7/8 +V); +48V on pin 1.
- Group: separate ±VA (summers) and ±VB (everything else), each through 10R PF.
- Master: ±VA through 10R PF, ±VB through 1R PF (phones), and ±VM through 10R PF to the meter bridge. DC input tags with 1N4002 clamps and 100 µF.
- No rail voltage is given.
- vs ours: similar per-module filtering. Ours has separate logic rails and a logic ground; theirs has none.

**T20 Op-amps / stability.** **Read:**
- Op-amps: TL072 in the preamp (revision 5.1 changed IC2 from 5532 to TL072), EQ, re-inverters and monitors; 5532 in the fader / mute stages, summers and balanced drivers; LM339 for the meters.
- 22 pF across almost every feedback resistor, 100 pF on the master summers.
- 47R build-outs on outputs, 22R on insert sends, ferrite beads at the summing inputs.
- vs ours: the same practice. Our parts are quieter.

**T21 Levels.** Internal level is −2 dBu (annotations); PFL / AFL −8 dBu; rails unknown.
- vs ours: our 0 dBu internal level is ~2 dB hotter.

**T22 Construction.** **Read:**
- Mono channel PCB; jacks / XLRs on a 4-channel connector board, each channel on a 12-way flexi.
- 34-way main ribbon carrying 8 aux, 8 groups, L/R, PFL, PFL_DC, 4 mute groups, rails and +48V.
- Tinned-copper earth bus.
- Group PCB in two build variants, A (groups 1/2) and B (3/4), by fitted parts and jumpers.
- vs ours: the same concept: a jack board on a flexi, plus the ribbon and an earth harness.

## 4. Worth considering for goa-console
1. **Fader shut-off compensation** (master, p.16): 100K from the fader top into a ~10R in the post-amp's ground leg cancels the leak through the fader's end resistance.
   - Cost: two resistors (and a link) per fader.
   - Why: our strip post-amp has +10 dB of gain, so fader-off attenuation is the end resistance ÷ 10K, minus 10 dB.
   - Check first: measure a PTB0143's residual at the bottom stop on the sample. Add this only if it is under ~80 dB, and size the resistor from the measured end resistance. Its spread limits the gain to perhaps 10-20 dB.
2. **Mute groups on the ribbon:** four buses, diode-ORed into the channel's mute driver. We have spare pins (1, 3, 5, 7, 19), and muting sets of synths together is useful live.
   - Cost: 4 diodes per strip, plus keys.
   - Against: the 8 HP panel has no room for four more keys, so this realistically needs a jumper-per-strip group assignment and master keys only.
   - Optional.
3. **JFET-mute null injection** (an extra resistor from the shunted node into the stage's ground leg): **not recommended**. Its depth depends on each JFET's Ron, whereas our two-J112 cascade already gives ≈ 86 dB without trimming.
4. **Discrete cross-coupled 5532 output** (4K7 / 10K 1 %, feedback after the 47R): a known-good fallback if the DRV135 supply ever fails. It costs 8 resistors and a dual op-amp per output. Keep the DRV135.
5. **Nothing found where ours is weaker than this design.**
   - Their line input (mic pre plus a 1 % pad), unbuffered insert return, unbuffered pan, single-tap strip meter with no hold, polarised couplers, PFL squeezed to −8 dBu for a 4053, and lack of output relays and ground cancel are all below ours.
   - Their pre-EQ insert and fixed ~100 Hz lo-cut are live-sound / mic choices, not relevant here.
